About the bakery

What is Kooki Bakes?

Kooki Bakes is a student-owned bakery offering quality, freshly baked cookies in Las Piñas, Metro Manila.

Ordering

How do I place an inquiry?

Browse the menu, then use Contact & Inquire to share your flavor, quantity, preferred date and contact details. Payments are through GCash and BPI. No cash on delivery.

How far ahead should I inquire?

Message the bakery as soon as you plan to order. Confirm availability and the required lead time for your preferred date.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes. Message the bakery immediately to discuss changes or cancellation and confirm what is possible for your order.

Cookies

Where can I find prices and ingredient details?

The menu shows sample flavors and provisional prices. Please confirm final prices, sizes, ingredients and allergen information with the bakery on social media before ordering.

How should I store the cookies?

Storage instructions and shelf life are to confirm and depend on the final products. Ask for the appropriate guidance with your inquiry.

Fulfillment

Do you offer pickup or delivery?

Delivery is through Lalamove or Grab. The shipping fee is paid by the customer. Ask the bakery to confirm arrangements for your location.

Packaging

What packaging do you use?

The bakery uses compostable kraft boxes, unbleached liners and no single-use plastic packaging.
